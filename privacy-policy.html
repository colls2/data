Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Collekt does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. No account is required. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Data stored on your device

Your collection data (owned items, ratings, notes) is stored locally in a database on your device. It never leaves your device unless you use the Export feature to create a backup file yourself.

Network access

The app downloads game data (character and skin information) from a public CDN (jsDelivr / GitHub). Images are downloaded from community wiki sources and cached locally on your device. No personal data is sent with these requests.

Permissions

Internet access is used only to fetch game data and images. No other permissions are required.

Third-party content

Game data is sourced from community wikis. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/colls2/data.
